How the math works (simple words)
  1. Risk in USDT = balance × risk %.
    1,000 USDT × 1% = 10 USDT you accept to lose.
  2. Loss per coin at the stop = how far the price moves from entry to stop + the entry fee + the exit fee. A fee is the price × the fee rate (you pay it when you open and when you close).
    Which fee: opening = taker for a Market order, maker for a Limit order; closing at the stop = taker (a stop fills as a market order); closing at a TP = taker, or maker with "TP as limit order".
  3. Coins = risk in USDT ÷ loss per coin. Position size (USDT) = coins × entry price.
    Because fees are inside step 2, the loss at the stop, fees included, is exactly your risk.
  4. Size by "% of available" (instead of Risk %): margin = Available × the % you pick (Balance when Available is empty), position = margin × leverage, coins = position ÷ entry. Your risk is then whatever the stop makes it: loss at stop = coins × loss per coin, shown in USDT and as % of your balance — red above 2%. Near 100% the position is trimmed a little so the margin plus the opening fee still fit.
  5. Order in contracts: MEXC trades whole contracts (1 contract = 0.0001 BTC, 0.01 ETH or 0.1 SOL). Contracts = coins ÷ contract size, rounded down to MEXC's step — so the real risk ends up a little below your plan, never above. All results (position, margin, loss, profit, open risk) use this real order; the unrounded plan is a small note under Position size. Below MEXC's minimum (1 contract) you get a red warning.
  6. Margin = position size ÷ leverage. Leverage does not change how many USDT you win or lose — it only changes how much money is locked as margin, and how close liquidation is.
    Already in a trade? The margin (+ opening fee) must fit in Available if you fill it in, otherwise in your Balance. Your risk is always a % of the whole Balance.
  7. Profit at a take profit = coins × distance from entry to TP − entry fee − exit fee. Each TP is shown as if the whole position closes there.
  8. Reward-to-risk = profit at TP ÷ loss at stop (after fees). "Before fees" = TP distance ÷ stop distance — what you see on a chart.
  9. Take profit from a ratio (1.5R, 2R, 3R, Custom, Ladder) = the price where your profit after both fees is exactly R times your loss at the stop (also after fees). Because you pay a fee on the way out too, the TP sits a little further away than "R × the stop distance".
    LONG: TP = (entry × (1 + entry fee) + R × loss per coin) ÷ (1 − TP fee)  ·  SHORT: TP = (entry × (1 − entry fee) − R × loss per coin) ÷ (1 + TP fee)
    "Loss per coin" is from step 2. The box shows the TP rounded to MEXC's price step (BTC 0.1, ETH and SOL 0.01) in your favour, so the ratio is never below the one you picked.
  10. Stop loss from a take profit ("Stop from TP" + a ratio) is the same idea backwards. First the profit per coin at TP 1 after both fees: P = distance from entry to TP − entry fee − exit fee. You accept a loss of L = P ÷ R per coin. The stop is the price where the loss per coin, fees included, is exactly L.
    LONG: stop = (entry × (1 + entry fee) − L) ÷ (1 − stop fee)  ·  SHORT: stop = (entry × (1 − entry fee) + L) ÷ (1 + stop fee)
    If TP 1 is on the wrong side, or so close that the fees eat the profit, there is no such stop — you get a red warning instead. The stop is rounded to MEXC's price step towards the entry, in your favour.
  11. Breakeven = the price where the price move exactly pays both fees, if you close with a stop or market order (taker) — like when a stop moved to breakeven is hit.
    LONG: entry × (1 + entry fee) ÷ (1 − taker)  ·  SHORT: entry × (1 − entry fee) ÷ (1 + taker)
  12. Liquidation (approximate, isolated margin) = the price where your loss has eaten the margin down to the maintenance margin.
    LONG: entry × (1 − 1/leverage) ÷ (1 − MMR)  ·  SHORT: entry × (1 + 1/leverage) ÷ (1 + MMR)
    MMR = maintenance margin rate: MEXC's first-tier rate for the coin (BTC 0.1%, ETH 0.1%, SOL 0.23%) unless you change it. Exchanges add tiers and fees, so the real price differs a little. Keep your stop well before it.
  13. Minimum leverage (only when margin doesn't fit) = position ÷ (balance − opening fee), rounded up.
  14. Cautions (amber) are advice, not errors: tight stop = stop distance ÷ entry is below the minimum for the coin picked under Live price (BTC 0.30%, ETH 0.40%, SOL 0.60% unless you change them in Caution settings); high fees = fees at the stop ÷ total loss at the stop is above 10%; high margin = margin ÷ balance (or ÷ Available) is above 50%; total open risk = risk already open + this trade's loss at the stop, as % of the balance, above 5%.

Live prices come from MEXC's public futures feed (no account, no API key). Only the coin name is sent to MEXC — your balance and trade never leave this device. Live prices need internet; the calculator itself does not.

Not financial advice. Exchanges round position sizes and prices, and each one calculates liquidation a little differently — always double-check on the order screen.
