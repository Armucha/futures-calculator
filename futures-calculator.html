How the math works (simple words)
  1. Risk in USDT = balance × risk %.
    1,000 USDT × 1% = 10 USDT you accept to lose.
  2. Loss per coin at the stop = how far the price moves from entry to stop + the entry fee + the exit fee. A fee is the price × the fee rate (you pay it when you open and when you close).
  3. Coins = risk in USDT ÷ loss per coin. Position size (USDT) = coins × entry price.
    Because fees are inside step 2, the loss at the stop, fees included, is exactly your risk.
  4. Margin = position size ÷ leverage. Leverage does not change how many USDT you win or lose — it only changes how much money is locked as margin, and how close liquidation is.
  5. Profit at a take profit = coins × distance from entry to TP − entry fee − exit fee. Each TP is shown as if the whole position closes there.
  6. Reward-to-risk = profit at TP ÷ loss at stop (after fees). "Before fees" = TP distance ÷ stop distance — what you see on a chart.
  7. Take profit from a ratio (1.5R, 2R, 3R, Custom, Ladder) = the price where your profit after both fees is exactly R times your loss at the stop (also after fees). Because you pay a fee on the way out too, the TP sits a little further away than "R × the stop distance".
    LONG: TP = (entry × (1 + fee) + R × loss per coin) ÷ (1 − fee)  ·  SHORT: TP = (entry × (1 − fee) − R × loss per coin) ÷ (1 + fee)
    "Loss per coin" is from step 2. The box shows the TP rounded slightly in your favour, so the ratio is never below the one you picked.
  8. Stop loss from a take profit ("Stop from TP" + a ratio) is the same idea backwards. First the profit per coin at TP 1 after both fees: P = distance from entry to TP − entry fee − exit fee. You accept a loss of L = P ÷ R per coin. The stop is the price where the loss per coin, fees included, is exactly L.
    LONG: stop = (entry × (1 + fee) − L) ÷ (1 − fee)  ·  SHORT: stop = (entry × (1 − fee) + L) ÷ (1 + fee)
    If TP 1 is on the wrong side, or so close that the fees eat the profit, there is no such stop — you get a red warning instead. The stop is rounded slightly towards the entry, in your favour.
  9. Breakeven = the price where the price move exactly pays both fees.
    LONG: entry × (1 + fee) ÷ (1 − fee)  ·  SHORT: entry × (1 − fee) ÷ (1 + fee)
  10. Liquidation (approximate, isolated margin) = the price where your loss has eaten the margin down to the maintenance margin.
    LONG: entry × (1 − 1/leverage) ÷ (1 − MMR)  ·  SHORT: entry × (1 + 1/leverage) ÷ (1 + MMR)
    Exchanges add tiers and fees, so the real price differs a little. Keep your stop well before it.
  11. Minimum leverage (only when margin doesn't fit) = position ÷ (balance − opening fee), rounded up.

Live prices come from MEXC's public futures feed (no account, no API key). Only the coin name is sent to MEXC — your balance and trade never leave this device. Live prices need internet; the calculator itself does not.

Not financial advice. Exchanges round position sizes and prices, and each one calculates liquidation a little differently — always double-check on the order screen.
